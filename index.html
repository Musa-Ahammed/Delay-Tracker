THE CASE FOR A SMALL PILOT

Reduce uncertainty.
Measure the result.

A hypothesis to validate, not a claim of proven impact.

01 / SCOPE

One moment, one feature

Start with restaurant preparation delays. Show the latest reason, delivery estimate, and update status in one tracker.

02 / DEPENDENCIES

Reliable signals first

Restaurant and rider events, an ETA service, and support routing. If signals are missing, show uncertainty rather than inventing a cause.

03 / SUCCESS

Fewer status enquiries

Compare support contacts per delayed order with a control group. Check ETA accuracy, satisfaction, and cancellation rates alongside it.

Independent assessment concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Pathao.

Pathao Food and foodpanda are the intended comparison pair. No live comparative test has been completed; no claim of superiority is made.

Research basis and limitations

foodpanda's published delivery information describes estimated delivery times and factors such as peak periods, weather and traffic. Pathao publishes food-order support guidance. These sources establish context; they do not verify this concept's assumed gap in either current app. Kitchen backlog, order details and event times in this demo are simulated.
